## NOT verified — deliberately left out

- **Rates / prices:** no fixed public rates exist — Wikitravel notes the hotel has no fixed price, and no OTA price list could be verified. The page therefore shows "Rates on enquiry" everywhere and the form confirms rates by WhatsApp. No number was invented.
- **Exact room count:** an OTA feed says 16 guestrooms, but it traces to a single syndicated feed (LateRooms/tiket share it), so no count is stated on the page.
- **Other room-type names:** only "Deluxe Double Room, Mountain View" is published (LateRooms). The second card is a descriptive "Twin & Family Rooms", and the form also offers "Any available room".
- **Airport shuttle / room service / concierge:** appear only on one messy OTA listing (airpaz, which also misplaces the hotel in Jutal and contradicts itself on Wi-Fi) — left out as unreliable.
- **Hotel's social links:** the hotel has an active Facebook page (posts Oct 2026), but no URL was printed on the page beyond WhatsApp/phone, matching the Fort demo pattern.
- **Street address beyond Lake Road, Upper Naltar:** none published; none invented.
