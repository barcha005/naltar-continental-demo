About Us

Welcome to Naltar Continental

At Main Chowk in Naltar Bala, Naltar Continental is the valley's oldest hotel — a home in the mountains for travellers heading to the lakes, the ski slope and the high meadows of Naltar.

Guests come back for the setting: a wide green lawn for breakfast and lunch with the river running by, modern rooms alongside wood-panelled rooms that stay warm at night, and food that reviewers call the best in Naltar. Families settle into the cabins; groups fill the lawn tables under the peaks.

Naltar Valley, From Our Door

Coloured lakes, pine forests and a ski slope — Naltar packs a whole mountain holiday into one valley, about an hour's climb from the Karakoram Highway side.

Naltar is famous for its lakes: Satrangi, Feroza and the other small coloured lakes scattered through the upper valley's forests and meadows. A jeep track climbs from the village to the lakes — ask at the hotel and we can help arrange a jeep for the trip.

In winter the valley changes completely: the Naltar ski slope, home to Pakistan's winter sports, draws skiers to the same meadows that hold wildflowers in July.

The hotel sits at Main Chowk in Naltar Bala, beside the river — close enough to walk the village lanes and forest paths, and far enough up the valley to wake to pine forests and snow peaks.
